A chain of steps that never ends is named at boot (`[moss] a chain of steps that never ends: …`) and refused at integration intake — walked together with the host's actions, since a loop can close through the host's triggers. A loop the host has on its own does not refuse somebody else's bundle. `IntakeContext.hostActions` carries the host's actions; `intakeContextOf` fills it.
