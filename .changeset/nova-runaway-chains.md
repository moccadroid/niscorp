A chain of steps that never ends now stops. An `emit` is delivered on the next task (`setImmediate` in node, a `MessageChannel` post in browsers), not a microtask: a trigger that re-emits its own channel used to keep the event loop from ever turning, and under moss that froze every principal's shell, the socket and HTTP with it. Every chain is counted from the gesture that started it — an emit reaching a message trigger, a `reload`, a navigation mounting the action it opens are hops — and a chain past 64 hops deep or 1024 hops in total stops and reports `RUNAWAY_CHAIN` through `onError`. A `reload` asked for while one is running merges into it instead of starting a second mount. `MessageBus.publish` and `ChannelHandler` take an optional opaque `cause`.
