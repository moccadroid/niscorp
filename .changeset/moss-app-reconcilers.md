An app can hand moss the functions that reconcile its canvases: `NiscApp.reconcilers`, nova `Reconciler`s by name, and a canvas's `reconcile` naming one — in the app's shell and in a page's. Every shell moss builds holds them (the app's and each page's, the one a document is drawn from and the socket's), and a reconciler is handed the shell `session.shell` is, which refuses what would change what the shell is made of. The boot is refused where a canvas names a reconciler the app does not hand over. A manifest without `reconcilers`, and a canvas without `reconcile`, are read and served as before.
