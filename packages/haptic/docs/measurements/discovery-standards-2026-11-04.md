The fixed pipeline remains declaration → acquisition/source trials → captured assembly → schema → published Prism shaping → advisory review/repair → fresh trial → publication. JSON owns decisions, prompts, ceilings and repair. TypeScript supplies mechanisms and derived contracts. No engine, model setting, prompt, dependency, database, controller, planner or new DSL was introduced.

The original oversized phase-contract draft was rejected during review. It repeatedly validated forwarded blueprint/capture state. The corrected views require consumed paths and produced slices rather than an entire global state. This correction and its failed intermediate measurements remain recorded.

The final integration also caught unsupported-format outcomes entering the prepared-owner collection. Acquisition now enumerates first, retains only completed preparations and preserves original indices/raw captures. Unsupported or incapable frontiers have no draft rule. The strict owned-document contract was preserved. This is JSON-owned acquisition bookkeeping, not a new parser fallback or semantic policy.

- The same saved ten-row answer projects from **12,598,123 to 73,558 compact bytes**, retaining its artifact, schema, trial, verdict and useful diagnostics. Removed bytes are private working state, not extracted answer data. This evaluates the actual new public projection on a saved result; it is not a new discovery run.
- The final composed discovery program is **803,875 bytes**, versus **413,474** before. Stronger contracts have real cost. The excessive intermediate draft was **1,185,821 bytes**.
- Three recorded preparation samples before the final small failure-path correction are **482/377/298 ms before** and **956/926/852 ms after**. Preparation admits and compiles the discovery builder once before its model work. These samples are descriptive, not a controlled throughput benchmark. They do not measure finished artifact execution.
- The unchanged engine executes **1,000 sequential number-increment operations in 0.995 ms median**, over forty samples, excluding preparation and network transport. Calling those same validated operations directly takes 0.108 ms. The engine adds about 0.887 ms to interpret their saved order and references.
- All engine source files and both model/default profiles match the frozen baseline. Prompt/root data equality and settings equivalence are recorded separately.
